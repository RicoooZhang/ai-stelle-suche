# Write a cover letter

1. Require completed job analysis, company research, application strategy, and candidate fact sources.
2. Select one clear motivation, two or three most relevant verified experiences, the value for this role, a sourced company reason, and a concise close.
3. Use professional natural German or clear non-template English. Avoid empty adjectives, exaggerated enthusiasm, repetitive CV narration, and long sentences.
4. Do not invent achievements, metrics, projects, relationships, motivations, or product experience.
5. Save `cover-letter-de.md` or `cover-letter-en.md`, trace each factual claim, and validate blocked statuses.
6. Mark uncertainties for review outside the formal draft; do not leave `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` inside send-ready prose.

Draft only. Never email, upload, or submit.
