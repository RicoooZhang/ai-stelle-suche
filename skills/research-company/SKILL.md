# Research a company

1. Start from the official company website and German legal/entity pages; add authoritative or reputable secondary sources when useful.
2. Record URLs, publication/access dates, and confidence. Separate `CONFIRMED_FACT`, `REASONABLE_INFERENCE`, and `UNCONFIRMED`.
3. Fill the company schema in `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`; never infer ownership, China connection, ERP product, culture, or office presence without evidence.
4. Treat recent information as time-sensitive and verify it online when browsing is allowed.
5. Save one record under `data/companies/` and a job-specific view in `company-research.md`.
6. Stop on login or access restrictions and list the missing evidence.

Research only; do not contact the company.
