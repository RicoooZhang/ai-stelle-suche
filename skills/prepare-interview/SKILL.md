# Prepare an interview

1. Read the exact saved job description, company research, match analysis, CV/letter version, and feedback history.
2. Produce likely HR, ERP/application/process, stakeholder, logistics, language, and role-specific questions.
3. Map answers only to verified candidate examples. Use a STAR framework without inventing actions, results, numbers, or leadership.
4. Mark missing examples `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` and provide questions for the user to supply a real example.
5. Include honest bridge answers for real gaps plus German and English technical keywords.
6. Save `interview-prep.md`; distinguish research facts from inferences and note sources.

Do not contact interviewers or schedule meetings.
